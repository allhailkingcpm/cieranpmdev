About Me

I'm a final-year student completing a dual B.S. in Computer Science and Cybersecurity at Drew University, with a minor in Creative Arts & Technology. I'm also taking graduate-level cybersecurity coursework at NYU.

My background spans both sides of the stack — from vulnerability testing and IAM analysis to building full-stack web apps and deploying self-hosted infrastructure. I like understanding how systems work at a low level, which is why I've spent time writing assembly, building Game Boy ROMs, and maintaining a homelab running Proxmox and Linux.

Outside of academics, I'm the founder of U Matter, a nonprofit I've run since 2016 that has raised over $30,000 for community initiatives. I was recognized with a Presidential Service Award for Excellence.
